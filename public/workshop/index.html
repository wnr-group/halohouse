Questions

Before you book

Do I need any modelling experience?

No. The workshop is built for beginners. Just bring your confidence and willingness to learn.

When exactly are the 2 days?

Day 1 (training) is Sunday, 15 November 2026, 2–6 PM. Day 2 (professional photoshoot) happens in the same week, and the slot is fixed with you based on your availability.

Who can join? Is there an age limit?

Anyone interested can join. There's no age restriction, and it's open to all genders.

What do I need to bring?

For the shoot, bring outfits for your 2 looks. If you need help with costumes, we'll share a costume designer's contact during the workshop.

What will I receive after the shoot?

Up to 40 photographs across 2 professional looks to start your portfolio. Halo House also shares participant portfolios with its brand clients.

Will I get modelling work after this?

Halo House is a production house working with multiple clients and shares your portfolio with them. Any selection for an assignment is the client's decision, so work isn't guaranteed. This gives your portfolio real exposure.
